Questions.

Why does Flick ask for Accessibility?

To answer ⌘Tab and bring windows forward. macOS asks the first time you open it, and Flick's Settings shows whether it's allowed.

Why Screen Recording?

Only for the window previews. Without it, tiles show app icons instead. Nothing is recorded or saved.

Does Flick go online?

Never. It has no account, no analytics and no update checks. Nothing about your windows leaves your Mac.

Is it really free?

Yes, every feature, for good. It's open source under the MIT licence. If you'd like to say thanks, you can buy me a coffee.

Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

Instant Space jumps and reading the trackpad use parts of macOS the App Store doesn't allow. The first time you open it, macOS may ask you to allow it in System Settings, under Privacy & Security.

Can I build it myself?

Yes. Clone the repository and run ./build.sh --install. It needs Apple's Command Line Tools and nothing else.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with macOS 26 Tahoe or later. Moving windows between Spaces and jumping straight to another Space need macOS 27.

How do I remove it?

Turn off Open at Login in Flick's Settings, choose Quit Flick, then move Flick to the Bin. The built-in ⌘Tab comes back as soon as Flick quits.
